Why SmartSwitch?

A small forgotten switch can mean wasted energy.

Busy routines make it easy to leave appliances running. SmartSwitch is designed to make everyday appliance control more convenient.

😟

The everyday problem

People sometimes leave home without switching off lights, fans, or other appliances. This can lead to unnecessary electricity use and higher bills.

💡

A smarter approach

SmartSwitch is designed to let users control compatible appliances through a mobile app, helping them manage switches more conveniently.

What it can do

Useful features. Simple control.

Designed around the everyday needs of homeowners, tenants, and working professionals.

📲

Remote ON / OFF

Control compatible connected appliances from your smartphone, even when you are away.

⏰

Scheduling

Plan when selected appliances should switch on or off to suit your daily routine.

🔔

Helpful Alerts

📶

Wi-Fi Connectivity

Home Convenience

Energy-conscious Living

Simple by design

How SmartSwitch is intended to work

A straightforward experience from installation to everyday control.

1

Connect the device

A compatible SmartSwitch unit is installed with the selected electrical circuit by a qualified electrician.

2

Pair with the app

Connect the device to your Wi-Fi network and pair it with the SmartSwitch mobile app.

3

Control with ease

Use the app to switch compatible appliances on or off and set schedules where supported.
